Mentor Program (Weeks 1–3) — 11 Activities
✓ Activity Objective Who Leads Status
Week 1 — Foundation  ·  Days 2–5
Mentor Intro Meeting
Day 2 · 1:1 Video or In-Person · 30 min		 Break the ice, set expectations, and align on communication norms, meeting cadence, and goals for the 3 weeks ahead. Mentor leads
Shadow a Prospecting Block
Day 3 · Live or Recorded Call Block · 60 min		 Observe real outbound calls — tone, pacing, objection handling, and how the mentor recovers from rejection in real time. Mentor leads
Persona Perspective Session
Day 4 · Informal Conversation · 30 min		 Mentor shares real field stories about calling CNOs, BioMed directors, and CFOs — what the course teaches vs. what it actually feels like. Mentor leads
Week 1 Self-Reflection & Check-in
Day 5 · Self-Reflection + 1:1 · 20 min		 What did you learn? What's still confusing? What feels intimidating about Week 2? Mentee submits a 5-bullet reflection before the call. Mentee leads
Week 2 — Execution  ·  Days 7–10
Live Deal Walkthrough
Day 7 · Pipeline Review · 45 min		 Mentor walks through 1–2 active deals: how they got in, where it stands, what's blocking, and what the next move is. A real pipeline conversation — not a role play. Mentor leads
Co-Pilot a Discovery Call
Day 8 · Live Discovery Call · 45–60 min		 Mentee joins a real discovery call silently. Listen for question structure, use of silence, and pain quantification. Do not speak unless the mentor asks. Mentor leads
Mentee Leads a Mock Discovery Call
Day 9 · Live Call Block · 60 min		 First time in the seat — mentee runs a mock or live cold call while the mentor listens silently. Mentor intervenes only to protect the relationship. Mentee leads
Mid-Program Pulse Survey
Day 10 · Written Survey · 10 min		 Structured check-in for both parties: is the relationship working? Does the mentee have what they need? Both submit separate surveys to enablement and the sales manager. Not optional. Both
Week 3 — Independence  ·  Days 13–15+
Account Strategy Session
Day 13 · Working Session · 60 min		 Co-build an outreach strategy for the mentee's top 3 target accounts — stakeholder mapping, messaging angles, cadence design, and timing. Mentee drives; mentor challenges. Both
Reverse Shadow — Full Call
Day 14 · Live Full Call · 45 min		 Mentee runs a complete call entirely independently. Mentor observes silently and completes the final call assessment. Both review it together immediately after — specific, honest, constructive. Mentee leads
Program Certification & Ongoing Relationship
Day 15+ · Structured Close · 30 min		 Mentee presents their 30-day independent action plan. Mentor and enablement co-sign and provide final guidance. The formal program ends; the relationship continues. Both + Enablement

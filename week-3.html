Supplementary Module

Pricing and Packaging Literacy

Know the catalog, understand the contract structure, and speak to commercial value without guessing.

[PLACEHOLDER — Standard terms: 3-Year MSRP (primary), 4-Year MSRP (longer term with better pricing), Annual Recurring (year-to-year). Default to multi-year when possible — it protects the customer from price increases and gives Kontakt.io predictable ARR. Always confirm term preference with your manager before quoting.]
[PLACEHOLDER — Location Services: Licensed Bed. Care Automation assets: per device/tag. Staff safety: per badge. Hand hygiene: per staff member or dispenser. Temp monitoring: per probe or sensor. Data services: per integration or tier. Professional services: per PS Day or event. Know the unit BEFORE the pricing conversation — "how many licensed beds?" is a required qualifier.]
[PLACEHOLDER — Back-of-napkin formula: Licensed Beds × per-bed rate = ballpark ARR. Use this at SAO stage to set expectations, not to quote. Capex-friendly framing: for CFOs — "this is a multi-year investment, amortized annually." Opex framing for CNOs/COOs: "predictable recurring line item, no large upfront capital." Match your framing to the persona and their budget structure.]
[PLACEHOLDER — Do not offer discounts without manager approval. Standard discount authority: [PLACEHOLDER — confirm with your manager]. Competitive situations may unlock additional discount via approvals. Never discount without understanding what you're trading for — extend term, expand scope, or accelerate close date. Discounting without a trade is a habit that follows you for the entire account lifecycle.]

Product Categories & SKU Reference

Location Services Bundles
Unit: Licensed Bed
[PLACEHOLDER — Hospital 360 Level 1: entry-level RTLS coverage (asset tracking foundation). Hospital 360 Level 2: expanded use case coverage. Hospital 360 Level 3: full platform with all location services enabled. Upsell path runs L1 → L2 → L3 as the customer expands use cases.]
Care Automation
Unit: Per device / per badge
[PLACEHOLDER — Care Assets Management: mini (limited assets) or maxi (full asset fleet). Supply Chain Agent: inventory automation — NO DISCOUNT ALLOWED. Staff Safe: SB3, SB3 mini, SB3+Cellular variants. Hand Hygiene: per staff member or per dispenser. Temp Monitoring: Pro, Lite, or probe-only tiers.]
Patient Use Cases
Unit: Per feature / per licensed bed
[PLACEHOLDER — Patient Duress Pull Station. In-Patient Auto Discharge / Room Turnover. Patient Flow Agent (success metric billing — see special rules). Patient Elopement / Rounding. Behavioral Health Observation. Access Agent Exam Rooms (success metric billing — see special rules).]
Data Services
Unit: Per integration / tier
[PLACEHOLDER — Curiosity Engine Analytics (platform-level reporting). EHR Basic / EHR Advanced (integration depth). API Stream (raw data access). Active-Passive Fast Recovery (disaster recovery — account-level only, see special rules). On-Prem Duress Redundant (local failover).]
Professional Services
Unit: Per PS Day or per event
[PLACEHOLDER — PS Day variants: Field Engineer, Project Manager, Developer. Onsite Install (hardware deployment). Travel & Shipment (logistics). PS Days are scoped at SOW stage — do not guess quantities. Loop in your SE or PS team for accurate scoping before quoting.]
Misc / Starter Kits
Unit: Per unit / per kit
[PLACEHOLDER — Indoor Navigation Basic / Advanced IPS (wayfinding). Sticker Tags (direct-only, see special rules). IV Pump Bracket (hardware accessory). Starter Kits (bundle for initial deployment — confirm current kit contents with your SE).]
SKUs with Special Pricing or Delivery Rules
  • Supply Chain Agent — No discount permitted under any circumstances. Flag immediately if a prospect requests it.
  • Patient Flow Agent — Success metric billing. Pricing is tied to measured outcomes — confirm billing structure with your manager before presenting.
  • Access Agent Exam Rooms — Success metric billing. Same rule as Patient Flow Agent.
  • Sticker Tags — Direct-only SKU. Cannot be sold through channel or GPO arrangements.
  • Active-Passive Fast Recovery — Account-level only. Applied at the account level, not per-opportunity. Confirm with RevOps before including in a quote.
[PLACEHOLDER — Task 1: For your most advanced open opportunity, identify which product categories are likely in scope, estimate licensed bed count, and write a one-sentence back-of-napkin ARR estimate. Share with your manager to calibrate. Task 2: Review the five special-rule SKUs. For each, write one sentence explaining when you would flag it in a deal conversation and to whom. Submit via Slack.]

Pricing and Packaging — Role-Specific Application

AE
[PLACEHOLDER — At SAO stage, build a back-of-napkin commercial estimate using licensed bed count × per-bed rate. Use this to set CFO-level budget expectations early — not to quote. Know which SKUs are in scope before your first pricing conversation, and never quote Supply Chain Agent with a discount. Always get manager approval before presenting any non-standard commercial structure.]
SDR
[PLACEHOLDER — When a prospect asks about pricing on a cold or early outreach call, redirect rather than estimate: "I'd rather make sure we understand your specific environment before talking numbers — that's exactly what the intro call is designed to do." Never quote or estimate pricing in prospecting conversations. Log any pricing questions in your call notes so the AE is prepared.]
CSM
[PLACEHOLDER — Know which SKUs your customer has purchased, the contract term, and whether any success metric billing SKUs (Patient Flow Agent, Access Agent Exam Rooms) are in their contract. Success metric billing renewals require outcome validation — coordinate with your AE and RevOps before the renewal conversation. Expansion starts with what's already in the contract, not a blank slate.]

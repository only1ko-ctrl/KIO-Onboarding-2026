How to Use This Program

Everything you need to know to navigate the 4-week onboarding program, understand how progression works, and get the most out of every day.

Program Philosophy

This is not a reading list. It is a ramp program — built around the principle that revenue professionals learn by doing, not by sitting through decks. Every day of this program is designed to put knowledge in your hands in a form you can use on a call, in a room, or in a deal.

Healthcare is a fundamentally different selling environment. Your buyers are not tech buyers. They operate on thin margins, navigate committees, answer to regulatory bodies, and manage the literal wellbeing of patients. This program is built around that reality — not generic SaaS sales methodology applied with a healthcare coat of paint.

The four-week arc is deliberate. Week 1 builds your foundation — who we are, what we sell, who we sell to, and why it matters. Week 2 builds your execution muscle — how to run a healthcare-specific discovery, navigate a buying committee, enable your champions, and manage a deal from first conversation to closed. Weeks 3 and 4 shift into simulation and live pipeline, where you practice before you perform and then build momentum in the real market.

The program gates are not obstacles. They exist because context compounds. You cannot have a credible conversation about the hospital buying cycle if you do not first understand why hospital margins are under structural pressure. You cannot enable a champion if you have not yet mapped the buying committee. Each day is a prerequisite for the next — and the knowledge checks exist to make sure the prerequisite is real, not just clicked through.

Who This Is For

New Reps

This site is your primary onboarding resource for the first four weeks. Start with Day 1 each morning. Work through the content in order — days are locked sequentially and will unlock as you complete each knowledge check.

Complete your learning objectives honestly — they are self-assessed, not graded, and exist to help you gauge real readiness. Take the knowledge checks seriously; wrong answers reset immediately and there are no penalties for retrying, but the goal is genuine understanding, not pattern-matching to the right answer.

When a day is complete, you will see a confirmation screen. Take a screenshot and send it to your enablement leader. That screenshot is your formal completion record.

Managers

Use the Master Status Tracker to monitor your rep's progress across all 20 days, the Mentor Program, and Milestones M1–M7 in a single view. Status values sync automatically from the week pages — no manual updating required on your end.

You will receive completion screenshots from your rep for each day they finish. These are the manual accountability mechanism — the program has no automated backend reporting.

Milestones require your active participation: mock calls, ramp plan reviews, live CRM demonstrations, and debrief conversations. Review the Milestones page to see what each one requires and what "good" looks like before each milestone comes due.

How a Day Works

Each day is a self-contained tab within the week page. Switching to a day's tab automatically marks it In Progress. At the end of the day's content, you will find a knowledge check. Passing it marks the day Complete, unlocks the next day's tab, and fires the completion confirmation screen.

Flip Cards

Click a card to flip it. The front shows a concept or term. The back shows how to use it in a real selling conversation. Flipping a card marks the day In Progress.

Learning Objectives

5 objectives per day. Click each one when you feel confident you can do it without the page in front of you. Self-assessed — not gated, not graded.

Accordions

Click a section header to expand deep-dive content. Used for persona profiles, framework breakdowns, playbook details, and process walkthroughs.

Reveal Stats

Key numbers are hidden behind a blur until you tap or click them. Reveals individual stats or all at once. Tests whether you know the numbers before looking.

Knowledge Check

5 multiple-choice questions at the end of each day. Scenario-based, not trivia. Wrong answers reset in half a second — retry immediately, unlimited times. Passing unlocks the next day.

Status Toggle

Each day has a status button: Not Started → In Progress → Complete. Updates automatically as you engage with the content and pass the quiz. Syncs to the Master Status Tracker.

Day 10 is Different

Day 10 — Sales Processes — contains 6 sequential modules, each with its own knowledge check. You must pass Module 1's quiz before Module 2 unlocks, Module 2 before Module 3, and so on. The day only marks Complete when all six module quizzes are passed. Modules are: Sales Stages & Deal Progression, SDR-to-AE Handoff, Territory Structure & Account Tiering, Quotes & Proposals, Strategic Account Plans, and Domino Tier Strategy.

Day 10 also includes a sequential Sales Stages walkthrough — 10 deal stages that unlock one at a time as you click "Reviewed." This is separate from the module quizzes and tracks your review of the full sales process from SQL to Closed Won.

How Progression Works

Each day's knowledge check is the gate to the next day. The next tab remains locked and greyed out until you pass. There is no time limit, no maximum attempts, and no penalty for wrong answers — but you must demonstrate real understanding to move forward. The nav dropdown at the top of every page also locks and unlocks alongside your progress, so you can always see where you are.
Day 9 does not have a knowledge check quiz. Day 10 unlocks when Day 9's status reaches Complete. You can mark Day 9 complete by clicking the "Continue to Day 10 →" button at the end of the day, or by manually clicking the status toggle to Complete. This is intentional — Day 9 is about absorbing the AI positioning framework; your completion signal is readiness, not quiz score.
Week 2 is locked until you complete all of Week 1. At the end of Day 5, a "Week 2 →" button becomes available once you pass the Day 5 quiz. Clicking it sets your Week 1 completion flag and takes you to Week 2. If you navigate to Week 2 without completing Week 1, a full-screen lock overlay will appear and redirect you back.
The Week 1 and Week 2 dropdown menus in the nav bar show all 10 days. Days you have not yet unlocked appear greyed out with a lock icon. As you complete each knowledge check, the corresponding nav item unlocks. This means you can use the nav to jump directly to any day you have already completed — useful when revisiting content or checking answers before a milestone demonstration.

Confirming Completion

Every time a day is marked Complete — whether by passing a quiz, clicking the Continue button, or manually updating the status toggle — a Day Complete confirmation modal appears on screen.

The modal shows your name, the day title, and the date of completion. Take a screenshot of this screen and send it to your enablement leader. This is how the program tracks completion — there is no automated backend.

Click "Got It — Continue" to dismiss the modal and proceed. If you accidentally close the modal before screenshotting, you can re-trigger it by manually clicking the day's status toggle to Complete on the week page or on the Status Tracker.

The modal includes
  • Day title (e.g., Day 3 — Platform & Use Cases)
  • Date of completion
  • Kontakt.io Revenue Enablement branding
  • Screenshot instruction
Send the screenshot to your enablement leader via Slack or email to confirm the day is done.

The Mentor Program

View Mentor Program

The Mentor Program runs in parallel with the core curriculum across Weeks 1–3. You will be paired with a top-performing rep who has at least one year of tenure. The program consists of 11 structured activities timed to specific days of the core program — not scheduled separately.

4 activities: Mentor Intro Meeting (Day 2), Shadow a Prospecting Block (Day 3), Persona Perspective Session (Day 4), Week 1 Self-Reflection & Check-in (Day 5). Your mentor leads these sessions and brings real field experience to complement the curriculum content.
4 activities: Live Deal Walkthrough (Day 7), Co-Pilot a Discovery Call (Day 8), Mentee Leads a Mock Discovery Call (Day 9), Mid-Program Pulse Survey (Day 10). The Day 10 survey is submitted by both you and your mentor separately to your enablement leader and manager — it is not optional.
3 activities: Account Strategy Session (Day 13), Reverse Shadow — Full Call (Day 14), Program Certification & Ongoing Relationship (Day 15+). At certification, you present your 30-day independent action plan to your mentor and enablement lead. Both co-sign your readiness.

Mentor activities are tracked via status toggles on the Mentor Program page and in the Mentor Program section of the Status Tracker. Both you and your mentor should update status as activities are completed.

The Master Status Tracker

Open Tracker

The Master Status Tracker is a single-page view of everything in the program: all 20 days (Weeks 1–4), the Mentor Program's 11 activities, and links to the Milestones detail page.

Status values on the tracker update automatically whenever you change them on the week pages, and vice versa. If you mark Day 3 complete on week-1.html, it will show as Complete on the tracker the next time you open it — no manual updating required.
Weeks 1 & 2: All 10 days with topic summaries, milestone badges, and status toggles. Weeks 3 & 4: Locked with "Coming Soon" overlays until content is live. Mentor Program: All 11 activities in week groups with individual status toggles.
The "Reset All Progress" button (top right of the tracker, red border) clears all stored progress from your browser and reloads the page from scratch. Use this only if you intend to fully restart the program. This action cannot be undone.
Important: Your Progress Is Saved in Your Browser

All progress — day statuses, quiz completions, mentor activity statuses, and milestone statuses — is stored in your browser's local storage. This means:

  • Progress does not sync across different browsers or devices. Always use the same browser on the same device.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, cookies, or cache will reset your progress.
  • Your progress is private to your browser. Managers viewing the tracker on their own device will see their own blank state — completion is confirmed via screenshots sent directly to your enablement leader.

If you change devices or lose your progress, contact your enablement leader. They can verify your completion history from the screenshots you sent and help you pick up where you left off.

Tips for Getting the Most Out of This

1
Do one day per assigned day, not multiple days in one sitting. The content is sequenced intentionally. Rushing Day 4 immediately after Day 3 short-circuits the reflection that makes the knowledge stick — especially for persona mastery and discovery frameworks.
2
Screenshot the completion modal before clicking "Got It." Once dismissed, you can only re-trigger the modal by clicking your status toggle back to Not Started and then back to Complete. Save yourself the hassle — screenshot it when it appears.
3
Day 8 has a mandatory assignment. Before Day 9, you must submit a Google Doc to enablement covering a stakeholder map, buyer milestone assessment, multithreading outreach message, and a business case paragraph. Prepare it during or after Day 8 — you will be expected to present it at the start of Day 9 with your mentor or manager.
4
Use the Objection Library as a live reference. The Objection Library is accessible at any time from the Resources menu. It is not locked or gated. Use it before calls, during prep, or to sharpen your LAER responses after Day 7.
5
Wrong answers on knowledge checks are not punished — but notice them. If you are retrying the same question more than twice, stop and re-read the relevant content. The explanations that appear after a correct answer are often more useful than the question itself.
6
Mentor reflections and deliverables are as important as the quiz scores. The Mentor Program's written reflections — especially the 5-bullet self-reflection before Week 2 and the 30-day action plan at certification — are where real learning gets synthesized. Put effort into them.

Milestones M1–M7

Seven proof points distributed across 20 days. Each milestone must be demonstrated — not just completed — before the rep advances.

M1

Product Courses — Complete

Week 1
Enablement lead confirms product course completion; rep can discuss key product use cases without notes
Asset Tracking & Supply Chain Agent
Rapid Room Turnover & Access Agent
Rep has reviewed and completed all assigned product courses and notified their enablement lead via Slack. All course modules are marked complete.
M2

Value Quantification

Week 3
Value assessment + 15-minute presentation
Complete value presentation; build a mock business case for a fictional hospital; present to manager/enablement
Rep builds and presents a credible business case with quantified ROI for a fictional hospital, using real Kontakt.io metrics. Presentation is clear, defensible, and under 15 minutes.
M3

Discovery Competency

Week 2
Mock call graded on: problem depth, quantification, emotional resonance
Discovery intro training; complete mock discovery call w/ manager/enablement; debrief with manager/enablement
Rep leads a mock discovery call that goes beyond surface symptoms — they uncover the operational and emotional cost of the problem, quantify it with the prospect, and leave with a clear next step.
M4

Pipeline Creation

Week 3
AE Pipeline Milestones: First 50 calls made and 75 emails sent; 2–3 active SAOs; 1–2 discovery/demo
Build target account list (25–50 accounts) in CRM; send first outbound communications; review sequence performance with enablement
Rep has a live, working pipeline in Salesforce with real accounts, documented outreach activity, and at least 2 active SAOs progressing toward discovery or demo stage.
M5

Execution & Readiness

Week 3
Present ramp plan to manager. Plan includes: Target accounts, Outreach strategy, Pipeline goals
Complete ramp plan template; 1:1 with RevOps on territory data and lovable territory mapping
Rep presents a specific, grounded ramp plan with named accounts, a documented outreach strategy, and realistic pipeline goals for the first 90 days. Manager approves it as executable.
M6

Tech Stack & Process Fluency

Week 3–4
Demonstrate ability to: Log a call, Update opportunity stages, Create a sequence
Complete CRM admin walkthrough; pass CRM data quality checklist
Rep can live-demonstrate all three CRM actions correctly and without prompting. Their Salesforce pipeline passes the data quality checklist with no missing fields.
M7

First Live Customer Interaction

Week 4+
Lead or co-lead 1 live discovery call or intro meeting with a real prospect; debrief submitted within 24 hrs
Shadow 2 AE calls before leading own; debrief with manager/enablement using call recording
Rep leads or meaningfully co-leads a live call with a real prospect, submits a complete debrief within 24 hours, and manager/enablement signs off on readiness based on the recording review.

Peer Mentor Program

A 3-week structured peer relationship running parallel to the core program — building confidence, fluency, and deal process understanding through direct observation, real deal work, and coached practice.

Program Overview

3
Weeks
11
Activities
2–3 hrs
Per week / per person

Purpose

Build confidence, fluency, and deal process understanding through structured peer relationships and hands-on activities that the core curriculum alone can't provide.

Mentor Profile

Top-performing rep with 1+ year tenure and a demonstrated culture fit. Mentors model both the skills and the mindset.

How the Weeks Map

Week 1 — Foundation: Set expectations, observe real calls, build context.
Week 2 — Execution: See live deals, shadow discovery, take the wheel.
Week 3 — Independence: Build your own strategy, run your own call, certify.

For Mentees

Come to every session prepared. Your mentor's time is your most valuable resource in this program — show up with questions, observations, and your own thinking ready.

Day 2 Mentor leads

Mentor Intro Meeting

📹 1:1 Video or In-Person  ·  30 min

Objective

Break the ice, set expectations, and share the mentor's personal journey — both in their career and at Kontakt. Define communication norms, meeting cadence, and goals for the 3 weeks ahead.

Deliverable — Mentee

Submit 3 things you want to learn from your mentor by the end of the program. Share before your next session.

Day 3 Mentor leads

Shadow a Prospecting Block

📞 Live or Recorded Call Block  ·  60 min

Objective

Mentee observes real outbound calls — paying attention to tone, pacing, objection handling, and how the mentor recovers from rejection in real time. Listen more than you watch.

Deliverable — Mentee

Write down 3 specific observations from the session and share them with your mentor after the call. Be specific — not "good pacing" but "paused for 3 seconds after naming the pain before asking the question."

Day 4 Mentor leads

Persona Perspective Session

💬 Informal Conversation  ·  30 min

Objective

Mentor shares real stories from the field — what actually happens when you call a CNO vs. a BioMed director vs. a CFO. The course content tells you the framework; your mentor tells you what it's really like.

Deliverable — Mentee

Update your personal persona notes with the real-world nuance from this conversation. Where does the course content hold up? Where does reality differ?

Day 5 Mentee leads

Week 1 Self-Reflection & Check-in

📝 Self-Reflection + 1:1  ·  20 min

Objective

What did you learn this week? What's still confusing? What feels intimidating about Week 2? This is also your chance to submit open questions to enablement before moving forward.

Deliverable — Mentee

Write a 5-bullet reflection and send it to your mentor before your check-in call. The call is a conversation — not a first read.

Move from observation to participation. This week, the mentee sees a live deal in motion, co-pilots a real discovery call, and then takes the wheel themselves for the first time.

Day 7 Mentor leads

Live Deal Walkthrough

📊 Pipeline Review  ·  45 min

Objective

Mentor walks through 1–2 active deals: how they got in the account, where the deal stands today, what's blocking progress, and what the next move is. This is a real pipeline conversation — not a role play.

Deliverable — Mentee

Map one of the deals using the buying committee framework: identify the Economic Buyer, Champion, Technical Evaluator, and Procurement contact — as best you can from what you heard. Discuss your map with your mentor.

Day 8 Mentor leads

Co-Pilot a Discovery Call

📞 Live Discovery Call  ·  45–60 min

Objective

Mentee joins a real discovery call silently. Listen for how the mentor structures questions, uses silence, and quantifies pain. Do not speak. Do not introduce yourself unless the mentor asks you to.

Deliverable — Mentee

Complete a call scorecard in real time during the session. Submit the scorecard to enablement directly after the call — before debriefing with your mentor.

Day 9 Mentee leads

Mentee Leads a Mock Discovery Call

🎤 Live Call Block  ·  60 min

Objective

Mentee runs a mock discovery call (or live cold calls) while the mentor listens silently. This is the first time the mentee is in the seat. The mentor does not intervene unless it's necessary to protect the relationship.

Deliverable — Mentee

After the session, write or update your own call scripts based on what you experienced today — the things that worked, the moments you got stuck, and what you'd change. Keep them.

Day 10 Both

Mid-Program Pulse Survey

📋 Written Survey  ·  10 min

Objective

A structured check-in for both parties: Is the relationship working? Is the mentee getting what they need? Does the mentor need any support from enablement going into Week 3?

Deliverable — Both

Both mentor and mentee submit separate pulse surveys to enablement and the sales manager. Two surveys — one for each person. This is not optional.

The mentee takes full ownership. Build a real account strategy, run a fully independent call, and certify out of the program with a 30-day plan the mentor and enablement both sign off on.

Day 13 Both

Account Strategy Session

🎯 Working Session  ·  60 min

Objective

Mentor and mentee co-build an outreach strategy for the mentee's top 3 target accounts — covering stakeholder mapping, messaging angles, cadence design, and timing. The mentee drives. The mentor challenges.

Deliverable — Mentee

Leave the session with a documented outreach plan for all 3 accounts — reviewed and approved by your mentor before you execute it.

Day 14 Mentee leads

Reverse Shadow — Full Call, Mentor Watches

🎤 Live Full Call  ·  45 min

Objective

Mentee runs a complete call entirely independently. The mentor observes silently and intervenes only if absolutely necessary to protect the relationship. This is the real test of what the 3 weeks have built.

Deliverable — Mentor

Mentor completes the final call assessment during the session. Both mentor and mentee review it together immediately after — specific, honest, and constructive.

Day 15+ Both + Enablement

Program Certification & Ongoing Relationship

🎓 Structured Close  ·  30 min

Objective

Reflect on the full journey together. The mentee shares their key learnings and growth moments. The mentor shares their observations of the mentee's development. Enablement facilitates.

Deliverable — Mentee

Present your 30-day independent action plan. Mentor and enablement co-sign and provide final guidance. This plan is yours to execute — without a mentor watching over your shoulder.

What Makes a Great Mentor in This Program

Shows real deals, not demos

A mentor who opens their live pipeline and talks honestly about what's blocking a deal teaches more in 45 minutes than any course module.

Lets the mentee fail safely

The reverse shadow is not a performance review. It's a learning environment. A mentor who intervenes too early robs the mentee of the experience that sticks.

Gives specific feedback

"That was good" is not coaching. "When the prospect said X, you missed the follow-up question — here's why that matters" is coaching.

Shows up on time, every time

A mentor who cancels or shows up unprepared signals that the program — and the mentee — doesn't matter. Reliability is itself a lesson in professionalism.

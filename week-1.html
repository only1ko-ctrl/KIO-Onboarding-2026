Using AI the Rom Way

Rom's 2026 GTM Operating System is AI-first. These are the 10 ways he expects you to use AI as a daily co-pilot — not just for writing, but for thinking, planning, and executing at a higher level. The tool: ChatGPT or Claude.

Ask Rom Anything — Open the Rom GPT

Bring messy context. AI helps you turn it into a plan. Good prompts:

  • "Here is what I know about this account. What is the real wedge?"
  • "What would Rom say is the right play here?"
  • "Challenge my account strategy."
  • "What is the executive-level reframe?"
  • "What should I stop doing?"

For GTM topics, anchor directly to the 2026 GTM Operating System: top 300 U.S. IDNs, land-and-expand execution, AI-first productivity, territory dominance, and selling outcomes rather than products.

Do not walk into a call with generic discovery. That is amateur hour. Ask for:

  • "Build me a pre-call brief for [account/persona/use case]."
  • "Give me the top 5 statements this customer is likely to agree with."
  • "What are the 3 assumptions I should test?"
  • "What would make this meeting successful?"
  • "Give me a strong opening that creates urgency without sounding salesy."

When you say "Let's work on a SAP," that means the Strategic Account Plan — not the software company. Start with one question:

"What are the top 1–2 enterprise priorities you're hearing from this account, verbatim if you can?"

Then build field by field: customer strategy, challenger reframe, stakeholder map, initiatives, use cases, competitors, whitespace, 90-day action plan, and risk ledger. The SAP is a living plan tying account initiatives to measurable, time-boxed plays.

Ask AI:

  • "What is the challenger reframe for this buyer?"
  • "Make this about enterprise outcomes, not RTLS."
  • "Translate this from product language to CFO/CIO/COO language."

Give AI the persona, account, trigger, and desired outcome. Example:

"Write a 90-word email to a VP Periop at a 20-hospital IDN. The wedge is OR tray visibility. Make it about patient flow and capacity, not tracking."

AI can help turn an account insight into a sharp executive narrative: tray mismanagement creates surgical delays, OR underutilization, PACU congestion, and downstream patient-flow impact. Kontakt.io's role is the orchestration layer connecting Epic, Aesculap, and real-time movement data.

Use voice or text. Pitch to the AI. Then ask:

  • "Score this from 1–10."
  • "Where did I lose executive altitude?"
  • "What sounded generic?"
  • "What objection would the CIO raise?"
  • "Rewrite my answer like Rom: blunt, strategic, outcome-first."

Bring technical content and ask AI to turn it into a business conversation:

  • "Explain BLE + IR accuracy to a CIO in plain English."
  • "Turn this RFI answer into a 30-second executive response."
  • "What is our differentiation versus legacy RTLS?"
  • "Make this about orchestration, not infrastructure."

The key story: Kontakt.io is cloud-native, supports BLE/IR/Wi-Fi integrations, Epic/HL7/FHIR workflows, multiple use cases on shared infrastructure, and AI-driven workflow optimization.

Do not ask "Is this deal real?" Ask:

  • "Inspect this deal like a CRO."
  • "What evidence says this is Commit, Best Case, or fantasy?"
  • "What is the missing political path?"
  • "What needs to happen in the next 14 days?"
  • "What would kill this deal?"

Push on: executive sponsor, quantified pain, timeline, procurement path, technical validation, mutual action plan, competition, budget, and customer consequence of doing nothing. No happy ears. Happy ears are expensive.

What Is the Joint Commission?

The Joint Commission (TJC) is the independent, non-profit accreditation body for approximately 4,000 U.S. hospitals. Accreditation is not optional — losing it means losing the ability to bill Medicare and Medicaid, which would force closure within weeks. TJC conducts on-site surveys (which can be unannounced) and evaluates hospitals against hundreds of standards across safety, quality, and compliance.

When a hospital receives "findings" from a survey, they enter "Requirement to Improve" status — they must demonstrate corrective action within 45–60 days. This creates an immediate, funded buying event. A finding is a burning platform.

Key Standards Areas TJC Audits

  • Environment of Care (EC) — Equipment maintenance records, safety rounds
  • National Patient Safety Goals (NPSG) — Hand hygiene, fall prevention, medication safety
  • Life Safety (LS) — Evacuation plans, fire drills, emergency procedures
  • Infection Prevention & Control (IC) — Hand hygiene compliance tracking
  • HR / Staff Competency — Training documentation, staff credentials
  • Workplace Violence Prevention — Sentinel Event Alert #59 (staff safety mandates)

How Signal Becomes Insight: Under 5 Seconds — step through each stage

1
Tag Emits

A small battery-powered tag clipped to an IV pump, staff badge, or patient wristband broadcasts its unique ID every 1–3 seconds using BLE, IR, or UWB radio. No human action required — it's continuous and automatic.

2
Readers Capture

Ceiling-mounted sensors in every room detect the tag's broadcast, record the signal strength (RSSI), and timestamp the event to the millisecond. Multiple readers capture the same signal for triangulation accuracy.

3
Engine Calculates

The middleware engine receives signal data from multiple readers simultaneously, applies triangulation algorithms, and resolves the tag's position to zone, room, or sub-meter accuracy — depending on the technology deployed.

4
Platform Acts

Location data reaches the KIO Cloud in under a second. Business rules fire: floor map updates, geofence alerts trigger, utilization counters increment, workflow automations activate. No dashboard refresh — it's live.

5
Outcomes Delivered

A nurse finds the pump in 12 seconds instead of 40 minutes. A transporter is dispatched automatically. Epic is updated. A staff duress alert fires to security. The patient's care transition happens without a phone call. The entire cycle: under 5 seconds.
